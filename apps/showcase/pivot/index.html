AdaptTable demo — pivot tables

Grouping answers "what is the total per team". A pivot answers "what is the total per team per quarter" — the second dimension becomes columns that were never in the data. Put fields on either axis, choose what each cell computes, and the table rebuilds around them.

What you can drive here

  • Move a field onto the rows axis, the columns axis, or the measures list — with buttons, so it works without a mouse.
  • Choose each measure's aggregation: sum, average, count, min or max.
  • Nest two dimensions on an axis and collapse a subtotal to hide what sits under it. Collapsing never changes the arithmetic.
  • Share the pivot as a link — the whole configuration lives in the URL.
