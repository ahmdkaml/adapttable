AdaptTable demo — Arabic, Hebrew and right-to-left layout

The same table in a right-to-left document. RTL here is a layout direction, not a stylesheet flip: column pinning is logical, so a column pinned to the start pins to the right in Arabic, and filter popovers anchor and flip from the correct edge instead of opening off-screen.

What you can drive here

  • Switch between English, Arabic and Hebrew and watch direction follow.
  • Pinned columns hold the logical start and end, not a hard-coded left and right.
  • Filter popovers, column menus and drawers anchor from the correct side and flip when they would overflow.
  • Every string, including pagination, empty states and bulk-action labels, comes from the translation catalogue.
