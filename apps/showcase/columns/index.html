AdaptTable demo — column management

Everything a user expects to do to a column, without writing a column-settings panel: show and hide, reorder by drag, pin left or right, resize, and switch row density. Each kit renders the controls with its own native menu and drawer primitives, so the panel looks native in Mantine, MUI, Chakra, Ant Design, Radix and shadcn alike.

What you can drive here

  • Toggle visibility per column, with a reset to defaults.
  • Reorder columns and watch the header and body stay in step.
  • Pin to either edge — pinning is logical, not physical, so a pinned column stays on the correct side in right-to-left layouts.
  • Resize by drag, and switch between comfortable and compact density.
  • Save the arrangement as a named view and switch between saved views.
