React tree table

A tree grid is a different shape from a grouped table: the rows themselves nest. Point AdaptTable at a parent id and children indent under their parent, each branch gets a chevron that opens and closes it, and the whole expansion travels in the URL like every other piece of table state.

What you can drive here

  • Open and close branches, and watch the address bar follow.
  • Walk the hierarchy from the keyboard — arrows move between rows and levels, Enter toggles a branch.
  • Sort and filter without flattening: a branch keeps its shape, and a matching child keeps its parents visible.
  • Switch UI kit — the chevron, the indent and the focus ring are that kit's own, not a shared approximation.
