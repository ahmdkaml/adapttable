React table live updates

Data that arrives over a websocket is the case where most tables come apart: the array is replaced, the whole view is rebuilt, and the reader loses their sort, their scroll position and their selection every few seconds. AdaptTable takes updates as row patches instead, so only the rows that changed re-run search, filters and sort.

What you can drive here

  • Watch budgets change on a timer, one row at a time, with a feed listing each patch as it lands.
  • Sort by Budget and keep watching — rows re-order live, because the sort is re-applied to the patched values rather than abandoned.
  • Filter to a subset and see rows join and leave it as their values move across the boundary.
  • Tick a row and let the feed run: the selection is held by id, so it survives every update.
